---
name: Stillroom visual identity
description: Brand intent behind the white and restrained dusty-pink palette.
---

Keep white dominant, with restrained dusty-pink accents. The intended feel is
modern, artistic, minimal, editorial and gender-neutral, not decorative or
stereotypically feminine. Pale pink is for selective emphasis, not a full-page
pink wash.

**Why:** The user explicitly replaced the former aqua/teal direction with this
brand direction while requiring the existing identity and functionality to remain.

**How to apply:** Use darker pink variants for readable text and controls.
Preserve the existing logo shape and typography identity. Do not tint uploaded
artwork, photographs or video to make them match the interface palette.
